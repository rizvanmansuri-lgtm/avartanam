Automated Test Fixtures

Inconsistent Testing Results

High-Precision Fixtures

FAQs

How long does custom fixture development take?
Depending on the difficulty of the design, it usually takes us 10 to 15 business days from design approval to send the product.
Can you support on-site installation and commissioning?
On request, we send our own technicians to your site for the installation, commissioning and training of the system.
Do you offer revision or upgrade services for existing fixtures?
We also carry out upgrading and alterations to test fixtures so that they are suitable for both new designs and better testing methods.
What information do I need to provide to get a custom test fixture designed?
Generally, we start fixture design by obtaining the product schematic, 3D model or sample, list of test points/pins, test specifications and information on connected instruments.
Do your test fixtures support both manual and automated testing?
There are both manual and automated solutions available from us. Our experts will suggest the right method, based on the size of your production and how complicated your tests are.
Can your fixtures integrate with third-party test instruments?
Yes. Our fixtures link to NI, Keysight, GW Instek, and bespoke test platforms via USB, RS232, LAN, or GPIB.
Do you offer HIPOT or functional testing fixtures?
We make devices that assist with high-voltage testing, insulation resistance, leakage current and all appropriate operational testing for safety compliance.
Is there training given once the fixture arrives?
Absolutely, we offer instructions and detailed training for working, handling and maintaining the supplied fixture.
Do you provide mechanical-only test jigs (without electronics)?
We have the option to send just the mechanical-built part (the bed-of-nails or the enclosures) if you want to setup your own electronics and software.
