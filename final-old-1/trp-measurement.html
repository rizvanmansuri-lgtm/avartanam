Product TRP Measurement Service

Know your true radiated power — TRP testing for OTA performance certification.

Total Radiated Power (TRP) measurement in NABL-accredited OTA anechoic chamber using full 3D spherical scan. Measures TRP in dBm per CTIA and 3GPP TS 34.114 standards. Supports Bluetooth, Wi-Fi 2.4/5 GHz, LoRa, Zigbee, LTE, GPS, 2G/3G/4G/5G, and Sub-GHz bands from 370 MHz to 6 GHz. NABL-accredited test report provided.
