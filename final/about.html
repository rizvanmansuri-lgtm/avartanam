About Us

A trusted name in RF, EMI-EMC and environmental testing and certification for over a decade.

Our Story

Excellence in Testing Since 2015

Avartanam Test Labs was founded with a clear mission: to provide world-class RF, EMI-EMC, and environmental testing services that help manufacturers bring compliant wireless and IoT-based products to market efficiently and on time.

Over the past decade, we have grown from a focused pre-compliance facility into a NABL-accredited testing laboratory serving clients across India and internationally. Our anechoic chambers, reverberation chambers, and RF shielded rooms are staffed by experienced engineers dedicated to precision and integrity.

We maintain NABL accreditation and work with leading national and international certification bodies — BIS, WPC, FCC, CE, SRRC — to support your global market access needs.
